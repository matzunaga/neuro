About and credit

What you see
One reconstructed human neuron, H16.06.004.01.04.05, from the middle temporal gyrus, layer 3. The database lists it as having spiny dendrites.
Movement
Beads show packages carried along the axon, in real time: outward at 2 to 5 micrometers per second, back at 1 to 3 (source; the outward range is a stated maximum). The flash shows the direction of a signal, from dendrites to cell body to axon, slowed down enormously. Adult branches mostly hold their shape, so they stay still.
Where it came from
Tissue removed during a patient’s surgery for epilepsy and donated to research.
Colors
White: cell body and dendrites. Yellow: axon.
Data
Allen Cell Types Database. © Allen Institute for Brain Science. This cell’s record. Reuse is under the Allen Institute terms of use.
Drawing
The traced points give position and thickness. A circle is swept along each branch. Thin branches are widened so they show on a phone, and branches are thickened where they meet the cell body. The path was slightly simplified and smoothed.
Not from the data
The shape of the cell body’s surface (only its center and size are recorded), colors, the grainy surface texture, lighting, depth haze, floating specks, and the size, number and timing of the beads and flashes. No spines or inner parts are shown.
